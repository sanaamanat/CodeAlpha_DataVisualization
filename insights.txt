7. DISCOUNT AND PROFITABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------

The Discount vs Profit visualization is useful for identifying
whether higher discounts are associated with lower profitability.

Transactions with high discounts should be examined carefully,
especially when they result in negative profit.

The business should avoid excessive discounting when it does
not generate enough additional sales to compensate for the
reduction in profit.


8. KEY DATA STORY
------------------------------------------------------------

The business demonstrates strong overall growth, with sales
and profit reaching their highest levels in 2017.

Technology is the strongest category and the West is the
strongest region.

However, Furniture has relatively weak profitability despite
high sales, and several individual products generate large
losses.

Therefore, increasing revenue alone should not be the only
objective. Management should focus on improving profitability
through better product selection, pricing, discount control,
and regional strategy.


9. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Recommendation 1:
Continue investing in high-performing Technology products and
top-performing products because they contribute strongly to
both revenue and profit.

Recommendation 2:
Investigate Furniture pricing, discounts, and product costs
because the category produces high sales but comparatively low
profit.

Recommendation 3:
Review loss-making products and determine whether they should
be repriced, discounted less, promoted differently, or removed
from the product portfolio.

Recommendation 4:
Continue supporting the West and East regions while developing
strategies to improve sales and profitability in the South and
Central regions.

Recommendation 5:
Monitor discount levels carefully to prevent excessive
discounting from reducing profitability.

Recommendation 6:
Use the interactive dashboard regularly to monitor sales,
profit, products, categories, and regional performance.
